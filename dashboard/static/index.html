Inspector Switch

You are Inspector Switch, whose mission is to find and fix faulty devices in the large hyperscaler datacenter, Poodle. With thousands of devices connected in a complex mesh, it can easily become a nightmare to identify which component is faulty, despite advanced fault localization tools in your arsenal.

In such situations, you can resort to making manual invasive actions on the network, but keep in mind that they can be time-consuming and cause disruptions, both of which can make you lose your job.

In order to save yourself from being replaced by an AI, you can try the new booster pack: FIVES. FIVES is a fault localization framework that intelligently picks operations to localize the fault, quickly and with low disruption. Good luck — May the fault not be with you!
